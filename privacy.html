Privacy notice

PhantomClerk · last updated 16 November 2026

Who we are

PhantomClerk builds order-entry and invoice-matching automation for businesses. Contact: zi@phantomclerk.com.

Business contact details

If we emailed you, we used a business address your organisation publishes on its own website. We record the company name, that address, where and when we found it, and our correspondence. We use this only to offer our services to your business, on the basis of our legitimate interest in business-to-business outreach. We do not buy contact lists and do not share your details with anyone.

If you reply "no" or "unsubscribe", we stop immediately and keep only a minimal suppression record so we never contact that address again.

Documents you send us

If you share sample purchase orders or invoices for an accuracy test, we use them only to run that test and report the results to you. Redacted samples are welcome. We delete the samples and any extracted data within 30 days of sending the report, or sooner if you ask. Paid projects are covered by a written data processing agreement.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run our business, such as email hosting, cloud hosting and document-processing services. They process data only on our instructions. A current list is available on request and is included in every data processing agreement.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to our contacting you, by emailing zi@phantomclerk.com. UK residents can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
